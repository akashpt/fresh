## Executive Strategy

The fastest path to real customers is not "SEO only". The site needs to become a trustworthy product discovery engine:

1. Fix trust: clear brand name, About, Contact, FSSAI, return/shipping, reviews, WhatsApp, address, policies.
2. Fix product discoverability: product search, category pages, unique product descriptions, product schema, image alt text, sitemap.
3. Capture high-intent searches: "buy raw honey online", "cold pressed groundnut oil online", "premium dry fruits gift box", "organic health mix for adults", "homemade pickle online".
4. Improve conversion: strong product pages, images, trust badges, reviews, bundles, delivery promise, checkout simplicity.
5. Build authority: Google Business Profile, Merchant Center, review generation, local citations, food/wellness content, marketplace presence.

## Part 9: Google Shopping and Merchant Center

Google says free product listings can appear across Search, Images, Lens, YouTube, Gemini, Shopping tab, Maps, and Business Profile when eligible. Required product feed fields include id, title, link, image_link, price, availability, brand, condition, shipping, return policy, and identifiers where available.

Implementation:
1. Create Google Merchant Center.
2. Verify and claim pureauranaturals.in.
3. Add business information, shipping, return policy.
4. Create product feed from WordPress products.
5. Use customer-facing product URLs.
6. Keep price/availability consistent with website.
7. Add GTIN where available, otherwise brand + MPN/SKU.
8. Enable free listings.
9. Link Google Business Profile.
10. Track performance weekly.

## Part 10: Marketplaces

Amazon: Yes, best for trust and discovery. Use top SKUs and bundles. Add inserts that invite customers to join WhatsApp/community, not manipulative review requests.

Flipkart: Yes for price-sensitive buyers and Indian ecommerce visibility.

JioMart: Yes if grocery/natural foods fit logistics and margins.

Meesho: Test only for low-cost bundles; protect brand premium positioning.

Goal: marketplaces build awareness; website keeps higher-margin bundles, subscriptions, loyalty, and exclusive combos.
